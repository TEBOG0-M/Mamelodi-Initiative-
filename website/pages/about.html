Our mission

Creating opportunities through education and community.

Mamelodi Initiative works towards a future where young people have support, encouragement and room to discover their potential.

Our purpose

Learning is a community effort.

Young people thrive when they have access to learning support, positive role models and opportunities to participate. The Mamelodi Initiative brings these elements together through educational and youth-focused programmes.

This student website presents the initiative and its programme areas as a clear place for learners, families, volunteers and supporters to find information.
