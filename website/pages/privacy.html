Privacy

Privacy notice

This page explains the intended approach to personal information for this student-project website.

Draft notice for coursework. The organisation should review and approve this policy before public use. It is not legal advice.

Information that may be collected

When the website is connected to working forms or account features, it may process information such as a name, email address, account credentials, and the contents of an enquiry. Only information needed for the stated purpose should be requested.

How information should be used

Information should be used to respond to enquiries, manage accounts and provide requested information. It should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

Security and access

Passwords should be handled by a trusted authentication service and must never be stored as plain text. Access to personal information should be limited to authorised people, and appropriate safeguards should be applied.

Retention and requests

The organisation should define how long information is kept and provide a way for people to ask questions about their personal information or request corrections where applicable.

Contact

Before launch, add the organisation's verified privacy contact and confirm the final notice with the responsible organisation.
